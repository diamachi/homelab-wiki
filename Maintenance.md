# Maintenance

Suggested checklist; not evidence of tasks already completed:

- Record actual service names, image versions and persistence paths.
- Check free disk space and recent service failures.
- Review backups and perform a sample restore.
- Review access grants and remove unused credentials.
- Test VM and game-server startup after planned reboots.
- After Docker/libvirt network changes, verify RDP rule order and connectivity.
- Update private records first, then manually review a sanitized public revision.
